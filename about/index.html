About Trailerfeed

Trailerfeed is a single place to catch the newest official trailers for movies, TV shows and video games. The list updates once a day, newest first, so you can see what dropped without digging through a dozen channels.

Where the trailers come from

Every trailer is an official upload from a studio, streamer, network, game publisher or an established trailer channel such as IGN. We find them with the YouTube Data API and play them with YouTube's standard embedded player. We don't host, edit or re-upload any video, and all trailers remain the property of their owners.

A trailer only appears once it has picked up a minimum number of views, and fan edits, reactions and re-uploads are filtered out. New versions of a trailer, such as a second or final trailer, show up as they are released.

Movie and show details

Summaries and release dates come from The Movie Database (TMDB). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

How the site is funded

Trailerfeed is free to use. Some buttons, like “Get tickets” and “Watch on…”, may be affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you buy through them, at no extra cost to you. See our affiliate disclosure. We never place ads over the trailer player.
