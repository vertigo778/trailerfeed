The Trust

Watch The Trust on Netflix.

Sir Idris Elba joins forces with His Majesty King Charles III to mark 50 years of The King’s Trust and explore how life has changed for young people. Growing up in Hackney, Idris received a Trust grant that helped launch his acting career. As he reflects on his own extraordinary journey, the documentary also follows three young people pursuing their ambitions with The Trust's support and the challenges they face today. Filmed over the course of a year, the documentary captures how The Trust has helped more than 1.5 million young people around the world develop the confidence and skills they need for work – and offers an intimate and insightful portrait of the friendship it forged between two men united by a shared vision.
