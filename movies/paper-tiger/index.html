About Paper Tiger

Paper Tiger is a 1980s crime thriller from writer-director James Gray (The Immigrant, Ad Astra), set in Queens. Adam Driver plays Gary, a former cop, and Miles Teller plays his brother Irwin, an engineer. The brothers get pulled into a scheme tied to the Russian mob, with Scarlett Johansson as Irwin's wife, Hester.

The film premiered in competition at Cannes in May 2026 and opened the New York Film Festival in September before Neon released it in theaters in November.
