Mr. Irrelevant: The John Tuggle Story is based on the true story of John Tuggle, a running back from Cal who was the last player picked in the 1983 NFL Draft. That pick earns the nickname "Mr. Irrelevant". The New York Giants chose him, and he made the team as a special-teams player.

David Corenswet stars as Tuggle and Michael Shannon plays Giants coach Bill Parcells, with Isabel May and David Krumholtz co-starring. Jonathan Levine (Warm Bodies, 50/50) directs from a script by Nick Santora. Paramount releases it in theaters on Christmas Day.
