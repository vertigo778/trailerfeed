Avengers: Doomsday brings Anthony and Joe Russo back to direct their first Avengers film since Endgame, with Robert Downey Jr. returning to Marvel as the villain Victor von Doom. Stephen McFeely and Michael Waldron wrote the script.

The cast joins the Fantastic Four (Pedro Pascal, Vanessa Kirby, Joseph Quinn and Ebon Moss-Bachrach) with Avengers regulars including Chris Hemsworth, Anthony Mackie, Sebastian Stan, Letitia Wright, Paul Rudd and Florence Pugh. Patrick Stewart and Ian McKellen also return from the X-Men films. Marvel has kept the plot under wraps.

It opens the same December weekend as Dune: Part Three, so expect showtimes to sell out early, especially in IMAX and other premium formats.
