Dune: Part Three is Denis Villeneuve's final chapter of the story that began in Dune (2021) and Dune: Part Two (2024). It adapts Frank Herbert's second novel, Dune Messiah, and Villeneuve co-wrote it with Brian K. Vaughan.

Timothée Chalamet returns as Paul Atreides, now Emperor, alongside Zendaya as Chani, Florence Pugh as Princess Irulan, Anya Taylor-Joy as Alia, Javier Bardem as Stilgar and Rebecca Ferguson as Jessica. Jason Momoa is back as Duncan Idaho, and Robert Pattinson joins as Scytale.

The film opens in IMAX the same December weekend as Avengers: Doomsday. If you want the biggest screen, book early.
