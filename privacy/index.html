Privacy policy

Effective November 28, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit Trailer Feed (the “site”) and how it is used. The short version: we don't run accounts, we don't set our own cookies and we don't use analytics or advertising trackers. Some third-party services we rely on do collect data, as described below.

Information we collect

We don't ask for or store any personal information. There are no sign-ups, forms or comments.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

YouTube videos

Trailers are shown with embedded YouTube players, and this site uses YouTube API Services to find them. Players load from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com), but when you play a video YouTube and Google may collect information, set cookies or use similar technologies on your device. That collection is governed by Google, not by us:

  • YouTube Terms of Service. By watching trailers on this site you agree to be bound by them.
  • Google Privacy Policy

We don't access, store or share any of your YouTube or Google account data. If you've ever granted an app access to your Google account, you can review or revoke it on the Google security settings page.

Affiliate and partner links

Buttons such as “Get tickets” and “Watch on…” take you to other sites, such as Fandango or streaming services. Some may be affiliate links. When you click one, that site or its affiliate network (for example CJ Affiliate) may set cookies to record that you came from Trailer Feed and whether you made a purchase. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave our site. See our affiliate disclosure.

Movie data

Movie and show details come from The Movie Database (TMDB). We fetch this data on our own servers once a day, so your browser does not contact TMDB. Poster and thumbnail images load from YouTube.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can browse the trailer list without playing any video. Because we don't hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete, but you are welcome to contact us with any request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Questions about privacy? Email help@trailerfeed.io.
