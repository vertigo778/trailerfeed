Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2

Watch Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 on Netflix.

Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 takes place in 2078 Night City, a crime-filled metropolis dominated by cybernetic modifications, gang violence, and powerful corporations. Following attacks by David Martinez and V, Arasaka begins losing influence, allowing its rival Kang Tao to gain power. The story follows Roman Carax, a young filmmaker who becomes involved with several Night City residents: D, a vengeful netrunner; Talia Yang, a rebellious girl escaping her corporate life; and Weak Kingsley, a former edgerunner searching for purpose. Their lives become connected through a mysterious datashard containing unpredictable power, which attracts the attention of criminals and corporations alike.
